# सार्थक ऑनलाइन सेवा केंद्र — Treatment

Style: **Mid-century Cartoon** (`styles/midcentury-toon/STYLE.md`, kept whole). Film: 59.7 s, 1080×1920 vertical, 24 fps, Hindi voice and burned-in Hindi captions, −14 LUFS. Made for WhatsApp Status (60 s limit), Instagram/Facebook Reels and YouTube Shorts.

Source: the user's own script (6 scenes, dialogue, voice-over, on-screen service list, shop address and number). Their story, cast and messages are kept; lines are tightened to fit 60 s (the full detail moves onto the picture as detail tags).

## 1. Three candidate structures

**A. The user's drama, tightened.** Problem (a son juggling three errands for his family) → a friend's hand on his shoulder → the shop → five numbered services → relief and a blessing → address and number. The cast carries the warmth the script asks for (आत्मीय, मददगार, भरोसेमंद); the numbered services are exactly the style's "onboarding for a service" grammar; the end card is the style's "store notice".

**B. A stack that empties.** One morning, one family, a pile of five papers; every service stamps one paper done until the hands are empty. A list that counts down. Clear, but the friend, the shop's place and the emotional turn get squeezed out.

**C. Do / don't split.** The frame split into two colour blocks: the old way (bank queue, tehsil, PF office, wandering) against the new way (one counter). Strong comparison, but static, crowded in a vertical frame, and it loses the family.

**Chosen: A**, because the user wrote it and its people are what a WhatsApp audience in Budni will recognise; it borrows B's thread (the son's paper stack becomes one neat folder) and C's contrast, turned into the film's one native move: the tangle of routes from scene 1 straightens into the sunburst behind the shop.

Opening image: the son's arms, a teetering stack of papers, the mother's passbook slipping off (not a logo). Ending: the shop's name and number dead still in a sunburst made from the same paths he wandered. Score shape: a tense half-time walk that goes round in circles → silence → the band comes in warm with the friend's hand → one vibraphone chord per service → the circling D-minor bass straightened into a rising D-major scale for the payoff → a quiet cadence under the number.

## 2. Logline and arc

A young man buried under his family's paperwork — his mother's pension, his brother's Samagra and Sambal cards, his own stuck PF — learns from a friend that all of it gets done at one counter beside the Janpad Panchayat gate in Budni.

- **Setup** (0–7.75): papers slip; "कहाँ-कहाँ भटकूँ?"; his wandering drawn as a tangle of dotted routes between four offices.
- **Turn** (7.75): after a full silence, the friend's hand lands on his shoulder; the cold blue wipes to warm.
- **Development** (12.75–36.75): the shop, then five services, each opened through a numbered medallion, each solving one of the papers.
- **Ending** (36.75–59.7): the mother blesses him; the tangle straightens into a sunburst around the shop; name, address and number held still.

## 7. Cue map

- **Tempo** 120 BPM, 4/4, swung eighths; bar 1 starts at −0.25 (the film opens on its second beat). Bar *n* starts at −0.25 + 2(*n*−1). Section downbeats: friend 7.75 (bar 5), services 17.75 (bar 10), payoff in D major 41.75 (bar 22), CTA 45.75 (bar 24), last bar 57.75 (bar 30).
- **S1 problem, bars 1–4**: half-time; D minor; pizzicato jazz bass circles D–E–F–E | D–C♯–D–A (wandering); brushes; soft vibraphone Dm(add9); muted-trumpet sigh at 5.75. Everything stops at 6.95.
- **S2 friend, bars 5–7½**: Fmaj9 on the hand; walking bass in F; brushes in 2 then 4; flute answers the question; snap at 12.25; gliss into 12.75.
- **S3 shop, to bar 9**: the theme, 4 bars on flute (F Mixolydian, the flat 7th for a Khamaj colour), vibraphone comping, bongos enter at 15.75 in an eight-beat keherwa-like pattern.
- **S4 services, bars 10–19½**: one vibraphone chord per service, held for its step: 1 Fmaj9, 2 Dm9, 3 B♭maj9, 4 C9sus → C13, 5 Am7 → A7♭9 (which resolves, a long way round, into the payoff's D). Bass walks, bongos keep the eight-beat groove, ride sticks from step 3. Flute only between lines. One-beat stop-time at 18.00 for the thumb.
- **S5 relief and payoff, bars 19½–24**: 2-feel, B♭maj9 → Fmaj9 vibes tremolo under the blessing; music out 40.25; gliss 40.75; **D major at 41.75**: the S1 bass, which circled D–E–F–E–C♯, now climbs straight up the D-major scale, one note per straightened path (42.0–43.75, eighths), doubled by glockenspiel; Dmaj9 with a suspended-cymbal bell and a long flute A–F♯ at the sunburst (43.75).
- **S6 CTA, bars 24–30**: cadence in D under the name; vibes and bass only under address and number; vibes chord with each stamp (45.75, 54.75); final Dmaj9 at 58.75.
- Checks: every stamp, wipe and iris lands on its beat (a script compares picture events with the beat grid; target 0 ms).

## 9. Type, captions and on-screen text

- **Titles and numerals** (slab): Eczar ExtraBold for Devanagari, Alfa Slab One for Latin and digits, ink over an offset colour plate — the shop name, service titles, medallion numerals, the phone number.
- **Body, tags, captions** (geometric sans): Poppins 500/600 for Devanagari, Jost 500/600 for Latin.
- **Caption card**: paper slab, thin ink border with its colour cel offset, small turning stars at the ends, ~46 px, centred low (above the apps' bottom bars). One cue per line; the two long lines (son, friend) split in two at a phrase. Hold ≥ max(1.8 s, speech + 0.6 s).
- **Service header**: ink disc with a paper numeral, a small spaced "सेवा 1/5", the title on a cream plate; slides in on twos.
- **Detail tags** (star bullet, pop on the action beat): "बिना लंबी लाइन के", "किसी भी बैंक खाते में", "नाम सुधार · नया कार्ड", "KYC · बैलेंस चेक", "सभी सरकारी फॉर्म".

## 10. Native move and signature

**The payoff turns function into ornament** (STYLE §8): the dotted routes he wandered in scene 1 — bank, tehsil, Samagra office, PF office — return in the plan view, and one by one each crooked path pulls straight into a ray ending at one door, the shop by the Janpad gate; seen whole, the rays are a 1950s sunburst, which then stays behind the name and number. It sits on the emotional peak, right after the blessing and the silence, and the ending echoes the opening.

## 11. Against our demo

Compared with `styles/midcentury-toon/DEMO.md` after this treatment was written: **structure** (a family drama with a turn, not a product set-up guide), **opening** (papers slipping from a son's arms, not a box opening), **signature** (an iris from the shop's wall clock into a town plan where crooked paths straighten into a sunburst, not a pull-back over a cleaning route), **score shape** (half-time D minor → silence → F major → silence → the same bass line straightened into D major, not normal → double-time → half-time), **ending** (a dead-still card with the number, no iris-out) all differ; the camera grammar (medallion irises, call-outs) is shared because it is the style's own.
